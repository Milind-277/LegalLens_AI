SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of October 1, 2026, by and between Apex Tech Solutions ("Service Provider") and Horizon Enterprises ("Client").

1. SERVICES PROVIDED
Service Provider agrees to provide web development, maintenance, and marketing services as described in Exhibit A (the "Services").

2. TERM AND DURATION
This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and shall continue for a period of twenty-four (24) months. This Agreement will not automatically renew.

3. COMPENSATION
Client shall pay Service Provider a monthly fee of $7,500 for the Services. Payments are due within 30 days of receiving an invoice. If payment is late, a penalty of 10% of the outstanding balance will be applied.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of any proprietary information shared during the course of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years after termination.

5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by providing ninety (90) days prior written notice. If a party materially breaches this Agreement, the non-breaching party may terminate immediately if the breach is not cured within ten (10) days of notice.

6. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
In no event shall Service Provider's total liability exceed the total amount paid by Client under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

7. INDEMNIFICATION
Client agrees to indemnify and hold Service Provider harmless from any claims arising from Client's use of the Services.

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Any disputes arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in New York, New York.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All intellectual property created during the provision of Services shall belong exclusively to Client upon full payment of all fees.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement.
